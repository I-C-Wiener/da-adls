DataArt Agentic Development Hackathon

DataArt invites engineers to take part in a hands-on internal hackathon focused on agentic development — building real software where autonomous AI agents are part of the development workflow, not just assistants.

This is a practical experiment aimed at answering a key question: How do agent-based approaches perform in real engineering tasks, and what can be reused in delivery?

📅 Dates (choose the date that best fits your schedule):

Saturday — April 18th, 2026
Monday — April 20th, 2026
Why Participate?
You will:

gain hands-on experience with agentic workflows, beyond tutorials and demos
test where AI agents actually help (and where they don’t) in real development
experiment with non-trivial orchestration, automation, and reasoning
contribute insights that may influence future delivery approaches
get compensated for tooling and time
This is not a competition and not a demo exercise — it’s a controlled technical experiment.
